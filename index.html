7 daily food habits

Do these most days and consistency does the rest.

🌅

Start with protein

A protein-rich breakfast (eggs, yogurt, tofu) curbs cravings and steadies energy all morning.

🥦

Half your plate veggies

Fill half of every meal with vegetables for fiber, vitamins, and lasting fullness.

💧

Hydrate first

Drink a glass of water before each meal. Thirst is often mistaken for hunger.

🍓

Whole over processed

Choose whole foods over packaged snacks. Fewer ingredients, more nutrients.

🍽️

Eat mindfully

Slow down, chew well, and stop at 80% full. Your gut needs ~20 minutes to signal fullness.

🌰

Smart snacking

Pair protein with fiber — nuts, fruit, hummus — to avoid mid-afternoon crashes.

🌙

Finish early

Try to eat your last meal 2–3 hours before bed for better digestion and sleep.

Quick tips that stick

Tiny tweaks with an outsized payoff.

🛒 Shop the store perimeter — that's where fresh foods live.
🍱 Meal-prep 2–3 days ahead to dodge fast-food temptation.
🧂 Season with herbs and spices instead of extra salt.
🍫 Allow treats in moderation — restriction backfires.
🥄 Use smaller plates to keep portions in check.
😴 Sleep 7–8 hours; poor sleep spikes hunger hormones.
